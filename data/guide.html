OFFLINE ESP32-S3 DASHBOARD

Connect without internet.

The ESP32 creates its own Wi-Fi network and serves the dashboard from LittleFS. No college Wi-Fi, router, mobile data, cloud, or Raspberry Pi is required.

Offline mode: the dashboard only works while the phone/laptop is connected to the ESP32 network. If your phone reports “No internet”, select “Stay connected” or disable automatic switching to mobile data.

01 / Connect and open

  1. Power the ESP32-S3 and open Serial Monitor at 115200 baud.
  2. On your phone/laptop, open Wi-Fi and connect to ICAM-Classroom.
  3. Enter password ICAM@2026.
  4. Keep connected even if the device reports no internet.
  5. Open a browser and visit http://192.168.4.1.
  6. Live readings are fetched from /api/status once per second. Open http://192.168.4.1/api/status to inspect raw JSON.

02 / Folder structure

ICAM2/
├── ICAM2.ino
├── icam_web.h
└── data/
    ├── index.html
    ├── styles.css
    ├── app.js
    ├── guide.html
    └── README.md

Keep icam_web.h beside ICAM2.ino. Keep the website files in data/ so they are uploaded to LittleFS.

03 / Upload steps

  1. Back up your working sketch first.
  2. Open ICAM2.ino in Arduino IDE and select your working ESP32-S3 board, port and partition scheme.
  3. Install the DHT and Adafruit display libraries already required by your sketch, plus a LittleFS uploader compatible with your ESP32 core.
  4. Upload the sketch normally.
  5. Upload the data/ folder to LittleFS using the same partition layout. A filesystem upload replaces the existing filesystem, so back up files first.
  6. Reset the board, connect to the AP, and open http://192.168.4.1.

04 / Live fields

  • DHT22: temperature and humidity.
  • Dual IR sensors: counted students and occupied status.
  • LDR: digital ambient-light detection, not calibrated lux.
  • MQ-2: raw ADC and threshold status, not ppm.
  • ACS712: estimated current in A and mA based on firmware calibration.
  • Fan relay, LED relay and buzzer output states.

The dashboard is read-only. Existing ESP32 firmware controls outputs. Current accuracy depends on sensor calibration and wiring; displayed readings are not certified safety measurements.

05 / Troubleshooting

  • Wi-Fi network not visible: reset the ESP32 and check Serial Monitor for “ICAM OFFLINE DASHBOARD READY”.
  • 192.168.4.1 does not open: verify the device is still connected to ICAM-Classroom, and type http:// explicitly.
  • Page opens but no live values: open http://192.168.4.1/api/status. If it fails, check the LittleFS upload and partition layout.
  • Dashboard files missing: verify index.html, styles.css, and app.js are in the data/ folder and uploaded to LittleFS.

Network and safety notes

This is a local, unencrypted HTTP dashboard with no login. Keep the network private and do not expose it to the public internet. MQ-2 is a demonstration sensor, not a certified gas/fire alarm. Never rely on the dashboard for safety-critical alerts.
